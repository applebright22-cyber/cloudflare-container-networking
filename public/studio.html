Merchant payment connections

Connect your own PayPal Business REST app. Credentials are validated with PayPal and encrypted before storage. Choose Sandbox while testing; switch to Live only when ready to accept real payments. This connection is for payments to your store and is separate from Vampirequid subscription billing.

Checking PayPal connection…

A successful connection validates the REST app credentials. PayPal Client ID/Secret do not identify the PayPal login or verify an account email, so Studio will not display an unverified account identity. Customers pay the connected merchant directly at checkout.
